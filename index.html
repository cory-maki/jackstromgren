About the Practice

Twelve years on Lincoln Avenue

Jack Stromgren opened Stromgren Family Dentistry in Willow Glen after completing a general practice residency at Santa Clara Valley Medical Center, and he has practiced in the same San José neighborhood ever since. The clinical focus is restorative: crown and bridge work, implant placement and restoration, and oral appliance therapy for patients with obstructive sleep apnea who cannot tolerate CPAP.

A large share of the practice consists of adults who avoided treatment for years. Stromgren narrates every procedure before performing it and books appointments long enough that nobody is moved through the chair on a timer, which is why dental anxiety as a clinical problem comes up in almost every new patient conversation. The longer account of his training and practice history covers the residency years, the implant caseload, and the community work that has run alongside both.

Alongside the practice, Stromgren is the originating figure behind the Executive Protection Dental Plan, a dental insurance concept organized around four operational pillars borrowed from close-protection work.
